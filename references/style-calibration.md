# Style calibration

Use a small number of deliberate references per assignment. Read the actual passage, inspect the figure or page at final size, and identify what it lets the reader understand. Study a second reference when it solves a different problem. The figures, phrases, statistics, endorsements, and competition rules in the examples belong to their own projects.

## ReGenCity Grid Indonesia

Use the [full Word document](examples/ReGenCity_Grid_Indonesia.docx) for English technical proposals, especially a complex mechanism that needs to read as one proposal.

- **Introduction.** Follow the narrowing from urban electricity demand to the limits of grid capacity, then the recoverable losses in specific urban systems. Each example gives the next paragraph a concrete subject. Bring the proposed project in after the reader understands the gap that justifies it.
- **Sections 2.1 and 2.2.** Establish the scientific boundary and describe the whole system before drilling down. Notice how the architecture explanation distinguishes what a local node measures, what operators decide, and what the city layer coordinates. Follow one key piece of information or responsibility across those parts.
- **Section 2.3.** The technical mechanisms each have a name the reader can locate. Their component explanations contribute to the same system argument. This is a structure to adapt when the feature list is long or the interfaces are otherwise hard to see.
- **Implementation and appendices.** Study the method justifications, specific assumption links, and use of diagrams and quantitative exhibits. Link beside the claim the exhibit supports. Give the link a meaningful label and confirm its destination in the final file.

The reference serves technical architecture, paragraph development, and evidence navigation. Apply the current proposal's evidence requirements to absolute novelty, projections, hypothetical designs, and test status. Carry over the reference's design intent through clean paragraph styles, without inheriting its variable local overrides, custom metadata, section lengths, or administrative material.

## Gotong Sampah

Use the [full essay](examples/Gotong_Sampah.pdf) to see how an Indonesian innovation essay connects a social problem to a proposed operating arrangement. Physical page locators count from the first page of the PDF.

- **Physical pages 3–4, Pendahuluan.** The opening narrows from the waste problem to the people doing the collection and the barriers they face. Follow the way prior collection responses enter as context for a concrete gap.
- **Physical pages 4–6, Pembahasan.** The solution is understandable as a whole before its feature and operating diagrams are introduced. Figures 1–3 distinguish what the platform does, what the actors do, and how their actions sustain the movement of materials. Explain how each feature solves one problem in that chain. Give the reader a reason to care about the handoff.
- **Tahap Implementasi and Analisis Risiko Mitigasi.** The rollout visual gives the main narrative something to interpret. Keep phase outputs, responsibilities, and the next decision visible. Give risks a response and an owner where the assignment needs them.

The source includes repeated benefit claims, page gaps, multi-page appendices, and broad performance statements. Use the approved compact production style and the current entry's own evidence. The feature-diagram approach transfers even when the page budget calls for one shallow overview and a more detailed appendix.

## Research-style reasoning

The opening of [PI MT](examples/PI_MT_Research.pdf) moves from SDG discourse to how reports construct urgency and assign responsibility. The research question emerges once the relationship is visible. Its example of an urgent report illustrates how urgency can communicate both need and expected action. The move is useful in a proposal opening when two related concepts might otherwise be conflated.

Read paragraph openings and endings in order. A new paragraph can pick up a consequence of the previous one, introduce evidence that changes the reader's interpretation, or carry the same subject into a decision. An explicit purpose or causal sentence earns space when a reader could otherwise miss the point. Vary where that explanation appears and how visible it needs to be.

A research-style passage can synthesize evidence, define how an observation is known, and distinguish inference from measurement. Use that precision without importing a research paper's full methods structure into a short competition proposal. In a genuine qualitative research proposal, show what the method treats as evidence, how interpretation is situated, and why the chosen design can answer the research question.

## Lautara trial

The [latest accepted trial](examples/Lautara_Trial.pdf) is a compact output example developed in this project.

- **Pages 1–2.** The opening moves from sediment disturbance and coral exposure to evidence limits in Kodingareng. Gagasan introduces the whole innovation, then peta arus, penampang dasar laut, and berkas survei each get a functional explanation. The connecting idea is how a decision survives from the map to the field record.
- **Page 2.** The wide, shallow Gantt fits in the body next to the implementation reasoning. The risk discussion explains why regional model resolution and actual measurement conditions matter.
- **Pages 3–4.** Sections A and B share one page. Sections C and D share the next with the references. Body links and returns are part of the reading path. Diagram arrows show the proposed comparison with no measured direction implied.

**The paragraph repair to keep.** An early peta arus paragraph read as a series of interface events. The accepted version states the purpose first, then explains why controlling date and location makes the depth comparison interpretable. A station example shows what that comparison prompts the officer to measure. Other paragraphs use a different shape when their job differs. Open with the purpose when it helps, without forcing each feature into the same sentence pattern.

The trial answers a particular research-informed concept brief. Its number of features, proposed four-month schedule, station labels, and compact four-page result belong to that task. When a brief specifies four appendix pages as a minimum, preserve the count. When it sets an upper limit, fill each page with useful material and combine related exhibits when they fit.
