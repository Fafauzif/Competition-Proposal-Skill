# Writing and Editing

Read the current brief, the exact passages under revision, the author's comments, and any selected exemplars. Write for the reader of the actual submission. Use the current genre, language, and page limit to decide what belongs on the page.

## Get the argument onto the page

- In an innovation essay, narrow from a relevant broader condition to a specific problem through a short synthesis of literature. Explain why each change of scale or evidence matters. Use APA 7 author–date citations, unless the competition specifies a different style. Where the brief reserves the idea for *Gagasan*, finish the introduction by making the unresolved problem clear.
- At the first substantive introduction of an innovation, give a continuous paragraph explaining what it is, who uses or operates it, where and when it applies, what problem it addresses, and how it works. Introduce the major features in that paragraph. Then explain them one by one, in an order that makes the operating mechanism visible. Explain the link between each feature and the problem it resolves before listing interface actions.
- Develop the whole system through a concrete person, transaction, decision, or observation. Let the reader follow something across features and handoffs. Preserve substantive technical, methodological, or financial detail when it changes the case. Write the intended arrangement with confident verbs and put rollout, projections, and actual accomplishments in their correct factual frame.
- For innovation essays and BPC, use plain section and subsection names such as *Pendahuluan*, *Tahap Implementasi*, and *Analisis Risiko dan Mitigasi*. BCC can use insight-led headings and named solution pillars when they help the reader follow distinct findings or interventions. Use hierarchy for navigation and relevance. Preserve required structure from the guidebook or template.

## Edit for connected prose

Calibrate English technical prose against ReGenCity and Indonesian prose against the closest Essay Studio examples. Use two or three relevant passages to learn how the authors narrow an introduction, explain a complete innovation, unpack a feature, or join a finding to an interpretation. Learn the move and write fresh language for the current topic. Read [prose calibration](references/prose-calibration.md) for worked examples and a read-aloud check. In the complete bank, use the [private example index](../../references/README.md) and [passage guide](../../references/style-calibration.md) to find the original pages.

Make each paragraph do a substantive job: synthesize evidence, explain a purpose, follow an operating mechanism, interpret a result, or develop a tradeoff. Use explicit language when a reader needs to see why a design choice exists. Keep some sentences direct. Watch for strings of actor–action statements followed by repeated conditional clauses, repetitive connectors, forced contrasts, acronym lists, and uniform recap sentences. An action sequence is useful where the sequence is the evidence. Vary how other paragraphs develop.

Use formal Indonesian with clear syntax and precise terms, following PUEBI and project conventions. Prefer ordinary punctuation and connected sentences. Avoid em dashes. Break semicolon-heavy passages into sentences, and replace explanatory colons with prose when a colon would make the writing read like an outline. Avoid decorative titles, stacked labels, inflated claims, and generic AI phrasing. In chat, answer directly and use headings only when they help navigation. In a submission, keep conversational setup, production commentary, scores, and offers out of the artifact.

## Revise within the agreed scope

Read each comment with its anchored passage. Preserve accepted wording, decisions, agreed depth, data, and references. Apply a comment locally unless the user expressly applied it throughout the work. Ask before propagating a local comment to other passages or making a material assumption. Within scope, fix necessary dependencies such as a cross-reference or a heading made stale by the edit. Check them after reflow.

When compressing, remove repetitive summaries, repeated qualifications and redundant definitions first. Preserve the thesis, worked mechanism, decisive evidence, meaningful limitations and requested scope. Use space-efficient figures or an appendix for supporting detail the reader can inspect separately. Submit a polished, usable revision and report only material assumptions or unresolved issues outside it.
