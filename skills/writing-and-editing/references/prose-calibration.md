# Prose calibration

Use adjacent paragraphs, figures, and captions to understand how an exemplar develops an argument. Rebuild the reasoning for the current topic. Approval, measured outcomes, evidence, partners, and claims of originality stay with the original project.

## Rewrite the information order

The miniature examples below were written for editorial calibration. They describe proposed arrangements and constructed cases.

**Feature as a chain of actions**

> Petugas memilih stasiun. Peta menampilkan arus. Jika kedalaman diubah, petugas membandingkan dua lapisan. Jika berbeda, petugas meminta survei.

**Feature as a design decision in use**

> Peta Lautara menahan lokasi dan tanggal ketika petugas mengganti kedalaman, sehingga perbedaan pada layar dapat ditelusuri ke lapisan yang sedang dibandingkan. Di sekitar terumbu, perbedaan arah antara dua lapisan memberi petugas alasan yang jelas untuk meminta pengukuran arus pada keduanya.

**Research synthesis**

> Kajian transportasi sedimen memberi kerangka untuk memahami pergerakan material, sementara penelitian lapangan mengaitkan kondisi setempat dengan paparan yang diterima terumbu. Kedua jenis bukti itu membantu menentukan titik yang patut disurvei. Arah arus pada kedalaman sasaran tetap perlu dicatat di titik pengukuran.

In an actual essay, replace the generic source descriptions with checked literature and fit the wording to what the sources establish. This paragraph expresses how studies relate and which decision follows, rather than using one sentence per abstract.

**Handoff with a specific consequence**

> Profil arus dan hasil laboratorium disimpan bersama waktu pengukuran, kedalaman aktual, dan kode sampel. Ketika petugas membandingkan dua kunjungan, catatan tersebut membantu menjelaskan apakah sebagian selisih hasil dapat berkaitan dengan perbedaan kondisi pengukuran.

**English technical prose**

> The comparison view holds station and date constant while the officer changes the displayed layer. At a reef-adjacent station, a shift in the modeled current direction gives the crew a specific reason to measure both depths. The resulting profile can show whether that difference occurs at the station itself.

Vary the prose by task. A compact specification can be plain and sequential. An explanation can open with purpose, a concrete consequence, a relationship found in the evidence, or a burden the design removes. Leave those options open rather than requiring one fixed pattern per paragraph.

## Read-aloud check

Read the opening, two consecutive feature paragraphs, and the transition to implementation as continuous prose. Mark any sentence that only repeats its heading or narrates a trivial UI action. Ask what the reader can now explain about the problem, the feature's purpose, or the consequence of using it. Check that each section earns the detail it carries and that the core argument survives with the appendix closed.
