# Case analysis and exhibits

## Issue tree and evidence

| Case question | Decompose it by | Check before proposing a solution |
|---|---|---|
| Where does margin decline? | Quantity, realized price, mix, variable/service costs, and fixed costs within capacity. | Compare like accounting periods and scopes. Isolate interaction and exceptional items where material. |
| Why are customers leaving? | The decision or handoff at which they stop, alternatives available, and the consequence. | Separate access, affordability, offer fit, and perceived value. Test whether one factor explains switching after comparable alternatives are considered. |
| Which operating change should go first? | Demand, actual bottleneck, handoff, delivery capacity, and measured completion. | Compare the observed distribution and exceptions. A shorter stage only improves throughput where it constrains the system. |
| Which investment creates value? | Ordinary practice, alternatives, incremental benefit/cost, capacity, and time to delivery. | Review budget and operating constraints before scoring. Test whether plausible changes reverse the recommendation. |

For each major branch, name the observation, best interpretation, credible rival, decision implication, and exact exhibit that substantiates the observation. Explicitly define the time, unit, population, and denominator. Surveys support patterns in the respondents. Interviews show how a choice plays out. Operations records locate events and cost at their recorded scope. Public market statistics establish external context. Where populations or periods differ, explain the difference before interpreting agreement or conflict.

## From recommendation to economics

Use one event definition from analysis through the appendix. For a hypothetical service case, a request goes through appointment confirmation, attendance, fulfillment, and payment. Any of these can be monitored. Count completed customer-paid work as the economic result if the proposal earns contribution at fulfillment. Estimate how many completions would occur under ordinary practice, subtract displaced business where material, and count the change once across initiatives.

| Model line | Minimum checks |
|---|---|
| Volume | Eligible demand, real reach, completion rate, capacity ceiling, timing, and ordinary-practice baseline. |
| Net revenue | Realized price, discounts, refunds, cannibalization, and recognition rule. |
| Contribution | Variable delivery and servicing costs, like-for-like units, and clear treatment of fixed costs. |
| Cash and funding | Timing of receipts, investment, working capital, and the funding dependencies in the rollout. |
| Sensitivity | At least the pivotal driver. Show the threshold or condition that would change the recommendation. |

Use one fair comparator at minimum where there is a meaningful choice. Choose frameworks by the decision they inform: 3C and segmentation for market choice, journey or process map for friction, profit tree for financial drivers, and feasibility gates before a weighted option score. If applying IFE/EFE/QSPM, disclose rating meanings, explicit weights, the basis for judgments, and whether plausible weights change the outcome. Use SWOT and TOWS only when evidence-backed factors produce a new decision. Avoid scoring unavailable evidence as zero.

## Build the supporting appendix

Link each sentence requiring support to its exact exhibit or subsection. Keep the main case understandable with appendices closed. Rank exhibits by their effect on judge or client decisions, not their quantity. Stock appendix jobs include:

- **Survey.** Sample and fielding summary, exact question and denominator, variable definitions, missing/excluded records, and a chart of the observed pattern. Check who answered any conditional questions.
- **Interviews and reviews.** Question or event, actor, trigger, mechanism, and exception or alternative. Preserve anonymity and present reported experience at its actual scope.
- **Benchmark.** Common units and comparison window, matched feature or business mechanism, and the limit on transferring the result.
- **Economics.** Baseline and incremental scenario, input definitions, resource or capacity limits, cash timeline, and a decision-relevant sensitivity.
- **Operations.** Flow with confirmation and exception, owner at each handoff, launch scope, and success/stop criteria if a test is necessary.

Use the authorized appendix scope and preserve the original when editing approved material. Draft the main exhibits where they explain the argument. Use the appendix to substantiate it. Apply the detailed visual treatment to the appendix by default. The main document follows the current brief and design.
