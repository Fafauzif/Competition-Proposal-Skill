# Provenance

The original TeX renderer, editable examples, TeX templates, stress fixture, and archival previews are drawn from the user-provided [Essay Studio](https://github.com/Fafauzif/Essay-Studio/tree/8fddfff968f492a1f13dc0bb7f97ca78bd09980a) toolkit. This private bank inherits the original archive's private-use status. No new distribution license is asserted for its sources or assets.

The fallback renderer, entry wrapper, and compact specimens are additions for this bank. The compact Gantt and paired sections use illustrative Lautara content. The correspondence between working input and document caption is part of the example. No illustration or fixture establishes an empirical finding.
