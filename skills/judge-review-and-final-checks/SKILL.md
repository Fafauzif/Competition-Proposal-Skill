# Judge Review and Final Checks

Review **the actual version the user will receive** against the current brief. A complete submission automatically gets the scored review loop and, when available and permitted, a separate critic. An authorized narrow edit gets a focused check of the changed material and adjacent regressions. Ask before extending the revision beyond the agreed scope.

1. Extract the required content, scope and depth, audience, rules, permitted assumptions, reference materials, and what counts as finished. Apply the named competition's rubric; where it is absent, use the criteria below without claiming they are official.
2. Produce or open a complete first attempt. Inspect the story and actual data, citations, calculations, render, navigation, and file structure as applicable.
3. Prefer a fresh independent critic for a full submission. Give the critic the actual artifact, raw brief and necessary source material. Ask for findings anchored to the page, claim, or exhibit, with severity and repair. Keep private files in the authorized environment. If no independent critic is available, do a distinct reviewer pass and describe it accurately. An agent who authored the content is not its independent reviewer.
4. Score each applicable dimension separately from 0 to 10 using observable evidence. The dimensions are task fulfillment, correctness, completeness, clarity/usability, and presentation/execution. A 5 needs substantial revision, 7 is usable with clear weaknesses, 8.5 is strong with minor issues, and 9 or higher needs exceptional evidence. A 10 is reserved for a genuine breakthrough. Use N/A only where the dimension truly does not apply. Scores do not replace rule compliance or verification.
5. Fix explicit omissions and materially wrong claims, calculations, or artifacts before substantive weaknesses and polish. Recheck what changed and anything affected downstream. Run up to four review-and-revision rounds. Finish early only when each applicable dimension has reached 8.5, every explicit requirement is met, and no known critical error remains. At the limit, deliver the strongest defensible result and disclose remaining material gaps rather than inflate a score.
6. Keep scores, reviewer notes, execution traces, and wording about production outside the submission. Handoff briefly with the exact file and only material limitations or owner actions.

Use the [review checklist](references/verification.md) for final-file and genre-specific checks. Review results should identify what was actually inspected and distinguish editorial judgments from verified properties.
