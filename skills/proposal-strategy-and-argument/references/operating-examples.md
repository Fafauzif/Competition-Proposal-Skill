# From Features to an Argument

## Follow one decision

In a coastal monitoring proposal, keep the date and station constant when comparing modeled layers. This makes the change in viewing depth intelligible. If layer direction differs in the example, explain why an officer would request a local profile, then show what the crew receives and how the readings return to the same record. The model provides the basis for a sampling question. Actual field observations determine what can be concluded about the station.

A workable paragraph sequence establishes the need for a baseline, introduces the complete operating model, then explains the map, the section view and the issued survey record individually. Each part has a purpose and an output in the next part's workflow. Reserve substantive feature explanations for the body even if an appendix has full interface views.

## Connect a business action to the completion event

A service proposal can follow a customer choosing a job, the branch confirming fitment and capacity, and the customer approving the installed price and slot. Where extra work arises, the adviser sends an updated scope and waits for authorization. Count a paid job when the promised service is completed and the counting rule's payment or recognition criteria are met. A booking request can be a useful funnel indicator. It cannot establish that the branch won back a paid job.

Check how the customer currently gets the task done and what part of the new experience is worth switching for. A benchmark can support a mechanism that another operator has used. Local outcome estimates still need local constraints and a counterfactual.

## Show the state the reader actually needs

An operating mockup can show “Request received” after intake and “Awaiting branch confirmation” while the workshop checks availability. Reserve “Confirmed” for a state with a real confirmation rule. If the draft includes captures, crop them around the decision and keep the evidence status accurate. Avoid substituting generated art for a supplied screenshot the user wants preserved.

## Identify genuine novelty

Give a concrete comparator and its strength first. Describe the change in workflow, authority, access, timing or experience that creates the proposal's advantage. A claim of incremental benefit needs its own evidence or explicit assumptions. A proposed design can be credible without a claim to being globally unprecedented.
