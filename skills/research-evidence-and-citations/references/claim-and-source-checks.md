# Claim and source checks

## Pick the right claim and unit

| Situation | Check before writing |
|---|---|
| Survey statistic | Who responded, how they were recruited, period, sample size, denominator for the item, response options and whether multiple selections were allowed. |
| Interview or review corpus | Corpus scope and collection window. Use a documented account to show how an issue occurs. Reserve a prevalence claim for a denominator and method that support it. |
| Model result | Inputs and version, spatial and temporal scale, baseline, resolution where material, and whether the model represents conditions at the point of decision. |
| Financial comparison | Currency, period, unit workload, fixed and variable costs, revenue versus contribution, timing of cash, and common boundaries for alternatives. |
| Impact forecast | Scenario inputs and sensitivity, one completion event, the fraction already expected without the proposal, attribution strength, and double counting. |
| Qualitative finding | Sampling and analytic context, interpretation and reflexive limits, and the substantive consequence for the question. Avoid universalizing a situated account. |
| Regulatory or competition rule | Authoritative source, date, applicability, stage or track, and any written clarification. A prior-stage rule may differ from the current one. |
| Screen or prototype | What the capture actually shows. Name whether it is a concept, local implementation, live interface, or documented test and what state was reached. |

Transfer citation-supported reasoning from reference essays only after checking the actual claim and source. An exemplar's numbers and outcomes are specific to that project. A familiar framework contributes when it answers a decision. Do not turn a qualitative comparison into fabricated numeric scores.

## Minimal private records

An evidence register can be a table or simple notes. Choose the smallest form that enables verification.

| Record | Fields |
|---|---|
| Source | Working key, meaningful identity, source version, URL or local locator, access date if relevant, exact locator, supported claim, scope and evidence boundary. |
| Claim | Draft location, claim wording and type, linked evidence or reasoning, units and denominator when relevant, disposition if evidence is missing. |
| Assumption or target | Definition, value or range, units, period, rationale, affected output, owner and sensitivity if material. |

In the submission, a public source citation or concise method note identifies the evidence. An internal appendix link identifies an exhibit. They have different jobs and can appear together at the same sentence when needed. Do not expose the private register or add conspicuous caveat prose merely because a field in it exists.

## Reproduction and disclosure

Recalculate headline quantities from available inputs. Reconcile totals, percentage points, units, periods, rounding, categories, and scenario labels across charts and prose. Test critical links or citations. With inaccessible evidence, record what was actually available and either qualify, substitute, or omit the blocked claim at the appropriate point. A partial reading or verification is not an independent reproduction.
