# Business Plan Competition

Build the case that this venture can acquire paying customers, deliver consistently, and finance the path to its next milestone. Fit the current organizer's format and criteria. Use plain navigational headings for written business plans. Treat examples as evidence of presentation choices and verify the current venture's facts independently.

## Show the venture first

- Open the summary with a plain definition of the venture, buyer and beneficiary, the problem it solves, the offer, and the credible economic or traction signal. If the venture serves Indonesian buyers, use relevant local payment, logistics, channel, regulatory, and seasonal conditions supported by current evidence.
- Follow one customer through discovery, purchase or authorization, fulfillment, payment, and continued use. Establish what the venture delivers, which parties do the work, and where it earns revenue. If the buyer contracts on behalf of beneficiaries, make that distinction explicit.
- Develop the user's starting idea. Explore alternatives when a consequential weakness appears. Use a fair comparator and explain what the chosen arrangement changes. Avoid counting expressions of interest, form entries, and paid or repeated transactions as equivalent traction.
- Translate the competition rubric into the claim each criterion needs, the supporting proof, and where the judge will see it. If the rubric is unavailable, label assessment provisional and avoid inventing its weights.

## Prove demand, delivery, and financial feasibility

1. **Customer and market.** Define the segment, actual use occasion, buyer decision, substitutes, and why the buyer switches. Bound TAM and SAM with real filters, then build SOM from reachable opportunities, conversion, retention or frequency, price, and fulfillment capacity. Keep people, transactions, recognized revenue, and gross transaction value distinct.
2. **Offer and go-to-market.** Specify what the buyer pays for, pricing evidence, channel and acquisition logic, operational delivery, and retention mechanism. Show who decides, buys, uses, and renews. Use a Business Model Canvas when the organizer requires it or its nine blocks clarify meaningful dependencies. It should summarize the supported model.
3. **Operations.** Connect forecasts to sourcing, staffing and trained capacity, lead times, fulfillment, quality control, payments, exceptions, and accountability. State launch milestones, owner, resource, and the condition that warrants spending or expanding.
4. **Finances.** Derive volume and revenue from assumptions the reader can audit. Separate variable costs, fixed costs, capex, taxes or platform fees when applicable, and working capital. Show unit contribution, operating performance, cash needs and funding, with horizon, scenario definitions, and capacity checks. Consult [Business model and cash](references/business-model-and-cash.md).
5. **Write what has actually been shown.** Present the complete designed venture with the confidence its reasoning earns. Distinguish model outputs, targets, and evidence of actual behavior. Include a prototype or test when the brief requires it or a key claim depends on it. Explain how the venture operates after that milestone.

Use [Proposal Strategy and Argument](../proposal-strategy-and-argument/SKILL.md), [Research, Evidence, and Citations](../research-evidence-and-citations/SKILL.md), [Implementation, Impact, and Feasibility](../implementation-impact-and-feasibility/SKILL.md), and [Charts, Figures, and Tables](../charts-figures-and-tables/SKILL.md) as the request warrants. Keep the value proposition, pivotal assumptions, and decisive financial conclusions in the body. Link the sentences that need more evidence to the exact exhibit. Show charts in wide, shallow arrangements in the counted body when they stay readable.

## Run the judge's check

Check consistency across the executive summary, proposed volumes, demand and acquisition model, costs, working capital, and funding timing. If operating profit and cash diverge, explain the reason. Review the actual submission using [Judge Review and Final Checks](../judge-review-and-final-checks/SKILL.md).
