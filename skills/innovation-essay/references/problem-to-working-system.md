# From problem to working system

## Paragraph jobs

| Where | Develop this relationship | Check |
|---|---|---|
| Opening | A consequential condition affects a defined population or setting. | Explain why the problem matters now. Use evidence that actually reaches the scope you claim. |
| Narrowing | Existing responses or conditions leave a specific unresolved problem. | Explain what the evidence establishes, what it leaves open, and the local decision the essay addresses. |
| First solution paragraph | Who uses the complete innovation, for what work, under what conditions, and how its parts combine. | Ask a fresh reader to explain the proposal with all figures and appendix pages closed. |
| Feature explanation | A feature changes a decision or handoff within that whole arrangement. | Show its purpose, action, output, and consequence in natural comparison, analysis, or example form. |
| Implementation | Requirements for the intended system become sequenced activities and accountable decisions. | Assign timing and owner, preserve realistic capacity, include a response to a meaningful failure, and tie any metric to its purpose. |
| Conclusion | The proposed mechanism answers the identified problem and supports a specific course of action. | Close on the substantive judgment. Keep detailed task lists in the rollout. |

Explain a system through a particular participant, decision, or material unit. A data workflow can follow one observation from capture to decision. A policy proposal can follow one case from eligibility to authorization and appeal. A physical design can follow an input through one operation to an output and fault response. Explain the comparison on common terms. Avoid inventing absolute novelty from an incomplete search.

## Example for explanatory voice

This is a constructed teaching example based on the Lautara trial. The station identifier illustrates the proposed interface. The example reports no field result.

> Lautara dirancang untuk membantu tim pemantau pesisir menentukan titik dan kedalaman survei sebelum pekerjaan laut dimulai. Di Kodingareng, peta arus memberi petugas dasar untuk memilih stasiun, sementara penampang dasar laut menunjukkan posisi sasaran pengukuran terhadap dasar perairan. Kedua pilihan masuk ke berkas survei yang diterbitkan penyelia. Kru kemudian dapat mengembalikan profil arus dan hasil pengujian sampel ke rencana yang sama, sehingga alasan pemilihan kedalaman tetap tersedia saat hasil ditafsirkan.
>
> Perbandingan pada peta mempertahankan lokasi dan tanggal ketika kedalaman diubah. Dengan begitu, petugas dapat menilai apakah estimasi arah arus berbeda pada dua lapisan di stasiun yang sama. Di S-01, perbedaan semacam ini akan memberi alasan untuk meminta profil arus setempat. Profil tersebut dapat memperlihatkan apakah pola yang diperkirakan model regional juga muncul pada pengukuran di stasiun.
>
> Berkas survei membawa pertanyaan itu ke lapangan. Instruksi untuk mengukur profil arus disimpan bersama stasiun dan kedalaman yang dimaksud. Bila tim juga ingin mengetahui konsentrasi sedimen tersuspensi, berkas yang sama dapat memuat permintaan sampel air. Waktu dan kedalaman aktual dicatat untuk kedua jenis hasil agar petugas dapat menilai apakah keduanya layak dibandingkan.

The first paragraph establishes the whole workflow. The second explains the comparison and the decision it supports. The third connects that decision to work the crew can carry out. Use Appendix links at the ends of individual supported sentences in the actual paper. Avoid repeating the opening pattern or future-state framing in each paragraph when the operation is already clear.

## Read the finished output

Keep the body justified with first-line indents for Indonesian submissions unless the current template specifies otherwise. Check how the final render handles long citations and nearby links. Scale or crop images to fit the intended content area while keeping important labels readable. Let a section begin wherever its heading and opening text fit. Keep a caption and figure together. Avoid decorative running headers, footers, callouts, and page-opening furniture that add no navigational value. Keep sources, assumptions, and support within the actual limits of the evidence and the guidebook.
